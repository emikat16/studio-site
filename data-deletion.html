Data deletion

Studio keeps your data on your own computer, so you control deleting it.

Remove Studio's access to an account

  1. In Studio, open Publishing and press Disconnect next to the platform. Studio revokes its token where the platform allows and deletes it from your computer.
  2. You can also remove access from the platform itself at any time:
    • Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites) → select Studio → Remove.
    • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions → Studio → Remove access.
    • Google / YouTube: myaccount.google.com/connections → Studio → Delete all connections.

Delete your videos and projects

Delete them in Studio, or delete Studio's data folder on your computer. Videos you already posted live on that platform; delete them there.

Ask us

We don't hold copies of your videos or tokens. If you believe we hold any data about you, email mediasimplifiednow@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days.
