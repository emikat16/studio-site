Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Studio is video editing software that runs on the creator's own computer ("your computer"). This policy explains what data Studio handles and what it does with it.

Your videos and footage

Your footage, edits and finished videos are stored on your computer. Studio does not upload them anywhere unless you choose to share a video to a platform and approve that post.

Connected platform accounts

When you connect YouTube, TikTok or Facebook, you sign in on that platform's own page; Studio never sees your password. The platform gives Studio an access token limited to the permissions you approved. Studio uses it only to:

  • upload a video you approved to your own account (YouTube: video upload; TikTok: video.upload, which sends the video to your TikTok inbox as a draft; Facebook: posting to a Page you manage), and check that upload's status;
  • show which account is connected (for example your TikTok display name or your Page's name, from TikTok's user.info.basic or the Pages you manage).

Tokens are stored in your computer's secure credential store (the macOS Keychain). They are not sent to us or to anyone else, and Studio does not read, collect or store your followers, messages, analytics or any other platform data.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent or share your data.
  • We don't use advertising trackers or analytics on this website or in Studio.
  • We don't post anything without your approval for that specific video.

Keeping and deleting data

Disconnecting a platform in Studio revokes its token where the platform allows and deletes it from your computer. Deleting a project or video in Studio deletes it from your computer. See Data deletion for every option, including removing Studio's access from the platform side.

Children

Studio is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and does not knowingly process their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
