Studio

A private video editing studio. Drop in raw phone footage, pick a style, and get a finished vertical video you can refine by hand or by chatting with an assistant.

What it does

  • Edits clips into a 1080×1920 video with a CapCut-style timeline: trim, reorder, split, text, colour and music.
  • Runs on the creator's own computer. Footage and edits stay there.
  • When the creator chooses Share and approves a post, Studio sends that one finished video to the creator's own connected account, using each platform's official API: YouTube (as a private upload), TikTok (to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft to finish in the TikTok app) and Facebook (to a Page the creator manages, as a draft).

How connecting works

The creator connects an account once, by signing in on the platform's own page and approving the permissions shown there. Studio never sees the password. Nothing is ever posted without the creator pressing Approve for that video, and the creator can disconnect at any time.
